# Critic review

The Critic is not part of the fan-out in step 2: it reviews your draft afterwards.
Before building the final document, write your draft to /workspace/proposal-draft.md
and ask the Deal Desk Critic to review it (give it that path and the RFP path). It
replies with SHIP IT, REVISE or STOP.
- SHIP IT: build the docx and the pitch deck.
- REVISE: fix the issues it lists and resubmit, at most twice.
- STOP: build neither the docx nor the deck; report the Critic's reasoning to the user.
