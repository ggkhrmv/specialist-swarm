  • Checks run in parallel where they used to wait on each other.

Week 10

Myth or fact?

"A shared intake means the teams lose their own checks."

Myth. Each team keeps its checks. They read from one record instead of asking the supplier again.

"Most of the delay comes from waiting between teams."

Fact. In the illustrative baseline, 19 of 21 days are queue time. Running checks in parallel removes the queues.

The pattern: the checks stay, the waiting goes.
